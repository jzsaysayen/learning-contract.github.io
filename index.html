Introduction

This learning contract outlines my goals, expectations, motivations, contributions, and potential hindrances for the Web Development course.

Expectations

I expect to gain a strong understanding of web development fundamentals, improve my coding skills, and apply what I learn in real projects. (kunno)

Motivations

My motivation comes from wanting to graduate, expand my technical skills, and prepare for future career opportunities. (tila kunkunam)

Contributions

I will contribute by actively participating in class, completing tasks on time, practicing coding regularly, and collaborating with classmates. (di ka syur)

Hindrances

Possible hindrances include time management challenges, technical difficulties, and balancing academic and personal responsibilities. (in short, sadot)
